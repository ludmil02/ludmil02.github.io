Self-organization in nonlinear wave turbulence

Rich Jordan
Worcester Polytechnic Institute

Abstract: In this talk, I will describe a statistical equilibrium model of self-organization in a class of nonintegrable nonlinear Schrodinger (NLS) equations. These equations provide natural prototypes for nonlinear dispersive wave turbulence. The primary result is that the statistically preferred state of such a system is a macroscopic coherent structure coupled with fine-scale random fluctuations or radiation. The coherent structure is coincides with a ground-state solution of the NLS equation. The predictions of the statistical model will be compared with direct numerical simulations of the NLS equation, and it will be demonstrated that the model captures the long-time average behavior of solutions remarkably well. Finally, some unanswered questions and open problems related to the statistical model will be discussed.
