Boundary Layer Associated with Incompressible Channel Flow with Injection and Suction

Xiaoming Wang
Iowa State

Abstract: We derive Prandtl type equation associated with the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations with porous boundary in a channel. We establish the validity of such approximation rigorously for a short period of time. In particular we prove that there is a boundary layer of the form exp(-Uz/nu) near the bottom of the channel where U is the vertical flux rate at the wall, z is the distance to the bottom of the channel and \nu is the kinematic viscosity. Stability of such profile will be discussed as well.
