Capturing Physically Relevant Solutions Beyond Singularity

Hailiang Liu

Iowa State University

Abstract:

In this talk I will take Burgers' equation as an example to explain two different solutions: the entropy/viscosity solution and the geometric(multi-valued) solution when it occurs in different physical contexts. After a short review of shock-capturing schemes for computing entropy solutions, I will present a novel level set method for computing the multi-valued solutions.
