It is well-known that creating effective quadrature schemes for meshless methods is an important problem. We discuss several issues concerning quadrature schemes for meshless method, and present two error estimates that show what error behavior can be expected under various hypotheses on the quadrature schemes. This is joint work with Ivo Babuška and Uday Banerjee.
