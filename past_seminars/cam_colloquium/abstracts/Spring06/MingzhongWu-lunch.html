Computational and Applied Mathematics Seminar, Department of Mathematics, Pennsylvania State University, 13 Jan. 2006

Spin Waves in Magnetic Thin Films


Mingzhong Wu

Department of Physics, Colorado State University, Fort Collins, Colorado 80523, USA



Spin waves in magnetic thin films provide a powerful and versatile “test bed” for the study of the fundamental properties of nonlinear waves in general. This presentation will give an introduction to spin waves in magnetic thin films. The presentation will review the excitation of spin waves in magnetic films, detection techniques, spin wave propagation properties, spin wave dispersion and nonlinearity, and spin wave envelope solitons.
