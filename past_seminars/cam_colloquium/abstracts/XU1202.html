Title:		Multilevel Iterative Methods for Large Scale Algebraic Systems

Speaker:	Jinchao Xu, Penn State

Abstract:

In this talk,  I will first give  a brief introduction to the  
multilevel iterative method based on space decomposition and   
subspace correction for solving large scale algebraic systems arising  
from the discretization of partial differential equations.  I will  
then talk about some recent results on robust and optimal multilevel  
iterative methods for singular and nearly singular systems with  
applications to linear elasticity, Maxwell equations, incompressible  
Newtonian as well as non-Newtonian flow equations.
