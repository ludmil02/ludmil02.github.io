A Fictitious Domain Method for Micromechanical Analysis of Composite Materials

Gregory J. Rodin
Texas Institute for Computational and Applied Mathematics
The University of Texas at Austin

Abstract: Fictitious Domain Method (FDM) is an effective tool for solving both linear and non-linear problems defined on domains with multiple boundaries, such as those commonly encountered in micromechanics of composite materials. The principal idea of the method is to simulate the composite body with fictitious forces applied to a body with a simple boundary. Finite element implementations of FDMs typically involve regular although non-matching meshes which are ideally suited for fast iterative solution strategies and parallel implementation. In this work we formulate a FDM for applicable to both linear and non-linear problems. Then we examine various issues related to accuracy, stability, convergence and efficiency of the method and present supporting numerical examples.
