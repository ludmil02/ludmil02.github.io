In the numerical simulation of incompressible flows, it is very
desirable to have the exact numerical conservation of physically
conserved quantities such as the energy and the helicity. The
conservation of quadratic moments not only provides a diagnostic
check, it also guarantees that the numerical scheme is nonlinearly
stable and free from excess numerical viscosity. Arakawa pioneered
the development of a numerical scheme that conserves energy and
enstrophy for 2-dimensional flows, but his method do not extend to
three dimensions. The creation of such schemes for the 3-dimensional
flow of fluids and plasmas is important in simulation of singular flow
behavior due to vortex stretching and reconnection. Although there is
at present no numerical method preserving both helicity and energy for
such flows, we were able develop a class of efficient numerical
methods based on the use of vorticity and a stream function for
incompressible fluids and plasmas for problems with a curvilinear
symmetry, such as pipe flow, axisymmetric flow, flow on a sphere, and
flow within a torus, cases in which the flow is effectively
2-dimensional. The divergence free constraint on the velocity and on
the magnetic field is thus trivially satisfied. We formulate a general
recipe to treat the nonlinear terms so that both energy and helicity
are conserved numerically. These conservation properties hold true
even at the pole singularity in the axisymmetric flow. For the
general 3-dimensional Magnetohydro-dynamic system, we developed a
MAC-Yee scheme preserving both energy and cross helicity (but not the
magnetic helicity). These methods should have wide applicability.
This is a joint work with Wei-Cheng Wang of National Tsing Hua
University, Taiwan.
