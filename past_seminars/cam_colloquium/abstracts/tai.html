Some numerical results for some inverse problems for reservoir problems

Xue-Cheng Tai University of Bergen

Abstract: Oil companies need to have a better characterization of the oil reservoirs. This can be done by formulating the problems as inverse problems using the production data of the oil reservoirs. The inverse problems are illposed. We will use a very simple model to explain the difficulties related to the problem. A brief review will be given for some literature approaches. In this work, we will propose some new formulations and present some numerical results.
