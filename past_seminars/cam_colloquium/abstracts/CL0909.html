On Viscoelastic Properties of Complex Fluids

Abstract: 
I will introduce an unified variational approach 
to model the viscoelastic properties of complex fluids.
The examples include liquid crystals, polymeric materials,
mixtures, magnetohydrodynamics, gel and foam and
common viscoelastic fluids. I will present some of the
analysis and numerical simulation results. Open problems
and challenges will also be addressed.
