The Tale of Two Olgas

Susan Friedlander

University of Illinois, Chicago, and I.A.S.

Abstract:

We discuss the careers and achievements of two important Russian mathematicians of the 20th Century, Olga Ladyzhenskaya and Olga Oleinik. They were students together in 1946 at Moscow State University and both were strongly influenced by Petrovsky and Gelfand to study PDE. Oleinik remained in Moscow and ultimately became the Head of the department of PDE. Ladyzhenskaya moved to Leningrad and became the leader at the Steklov Institute of a famous group in PDE and mathematical physics. Both proved fundamental theorems in PDE and both were challenged and inspired by questions in fluid dynamics.
