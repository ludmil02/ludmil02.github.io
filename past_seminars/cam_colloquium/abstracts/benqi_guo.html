Inverse approximation theorems for the p-version of finite element method.

Ben-Qi Guo
University of Manitoba

Abstract: Inverse approximation theorems indicate the regularity of the solution of the problem which we are computing, based on the observed convergence rate measured in the energy norm. A desired inverse approximation theorem result must exactly match the known direct approximation results. Selection and design of suitable spaces for the regularity is the key to achieve this goal. Since it has been proved that the N-width for functions in Jacobi-weighted spaces is achieved by polynomial approximation, it is clear that the inverse polynomal approximation theorems must be built on Jacobi-weighted spaces. During the talk, the sharpness of the approximation error estimation and the sharpness of regularity results, and the relation between two will be addressed in the framework of Jacobi-weighted spaces.
