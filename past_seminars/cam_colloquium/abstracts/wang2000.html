A Geometric Theory of 2-D Incompressible Flows and its Applications to Ocean Circulations

Shouhong Wang
Mathematics Department
Indiana University

Abstract: I shall present in this talk a geometric theory on the global structure and its transitions of 2-D incompressible flows, including a) structural and block stabilities of divergence-free vector fields, and b). structural bifurcation of the solutions of the 2D Navier-Stokes equations. The results and methods will be presented with simple schematic pictures. An application to the wind-driven, double gyre ocean circulations will also be given.
