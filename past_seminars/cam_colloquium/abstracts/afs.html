This presentation is about the analytical aspects of actuarial science. It
deals with concepts like credibility, mortality estimation, risk theory,
soft computing, and control theory from an actuarial perspective. It talks
about actuaries in the non-life and life fields, and their different senses
of urgency about bringing analytical theory into their practical work. It
talks about the current situation here and abroad. Finally, the talk ends
with a prognosis regarding the future.
