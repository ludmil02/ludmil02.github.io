Phase Transition in Dispersive Oscillations
Fei-Ran Tian, Ohio State University

It is known that the solution of the KdV equation is oscillatory when the dispersive parameter becomes small. The solution usually has a limit in the weak, i.e. average, sense as the dispersion goes to zero. This weak limit partitions space-time into regions in which the limit is locally governed by multiphase modulation equations.
