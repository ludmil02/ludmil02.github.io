Title: Small BV Solutions of Hyperbolic Non-cooperative Differential
Games

Speaker: Wen Shen

In this talk I will present some new results we obtained on small BV
solutions of hyperbolic non-cooperative differential games. The talk
will begin with a brief introduction to game theory, where I will
explain some baisc concepts such as zero-sum game, min-max theory,
n-person non-cooperative game, Nash Equilibrium etc. Then I will
introduce the differential game, and derive formally the Hamilton
Jacobian equations. I will state conditions for which this system is
strictly hyperbolic. In the positive case, I will explain the key
ideas of showing that the weak solution of a corresponding system of
conservation laws determines an n-tuple of feedback strategies. These
yield a Nash equilibrium solution to the non-cooperative differential
game.
