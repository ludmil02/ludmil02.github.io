Title: Approximation Results for Meshless Methods
Abstract: The approximation problem for meshless methods will be formu-
lated. Then the classical result of Strang and Fix will be stated, and applied
to the simplest example. The Strang-Fix result will also be related to the
approximation results of Babuška and of Bramble and Hilbert.
