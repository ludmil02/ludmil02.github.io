Application for participation

This summer school/workshop will be limited in size. Only researchers with considerable knowledge of multigrid methods are encouraged to apply for participation. If you are interested, please contact Dr. Ludmil Zikatanov (workshop coordinator, email: <ltz AT math DOT psu DOT edu>) with your contact information, a brief CV or a pointer to a web site that has some profile of your research. You are expected to pay certain amount (to be determined) of registration fee to defray the cost of materials and workshop organization.
